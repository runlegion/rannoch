The operator: "Rerun the toy but on the real code we have done". The original: T8 and T8b
(/Volumes/store/data/legion/toys/rannoch-toys/t8-neighborhood-eavesdrop, 2026-10-14), a relay
on the largest finding of T4's file, with its own miniature event, link and touch tables.

This round:
- Data: T4's file read immutably (1,000 findings, 2,916,404 statements, 100,001 voices,
  2,916,404 said links, 874,922 supports links), loaded into FR-RANNOCH-123's _nodes and _edges
  through rannoch main's real crates (queue, processor, events, store): 1,000 import events,
  each a range of statements, whose step writes the statement's place, its said edge and its
  supports edges; the rows are written by the append that carries each event's 2 (the toy
  append of round 2d).
- A judgment is one event whose step unlinks the statement's supports edge to the finding
  (not_supports) or links it again (supports); a new statement is one event writing its place,
  its said edge and its supports edge.
- The relay is seeded by describe(finding, [{"supports<-": ["place", {"said<-": []}]}]); after
  each append commit, each event's committed rows and the nodes they touch are handed to the
  relay once, and the relay folds those that touch its nodes with T8's fold (per-voice counts of
  active statements). Events enter _events directly with known ids and the queue is signalled,
  as rannoch's own processor scenarios do.
- Control: a full recompute from _nodes and _edges after every step.

R0 load: every import event reaches its 2, at 100,000 graph rows/s or more.
R1 (T8 P1): a judgment is one event; the graph holds one new row for it.

   event again changes nothing.
R8 (T8b P8): a new statement from a voice in the relay is delivered; statements +1, voices same.
R9 (T8b P9): a new statement from a new voice is delivered; both +1.
